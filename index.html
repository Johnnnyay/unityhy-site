PHILANTHROPYV

The Lea Foundation was established out of deep concern for children in rural China who remain in their hometowns while their parents migrate to urban areas for work. This situation often leads to significant emotional and psychological challenges, as these children experience isolation, neglect, and a lack of parental guidance. The absence of parents can hinder their emotional development and educational opportunities, perpetuating a cycle of poverty and social difficulties within these communities. This phenomenon raises serious concerns about the long-term effects on children's well-being and the stability of family structures.

The Foundation’s Returning Mothers program encourages women to return home by providing stable employment in traditional clothing production, promoting family unity, supporting left-behind children, and strengthening community ties. In collaboration with partners like China Life Insurance, the Suzhou Mingde Public Welfare Foundation, and individual philanthropists, the program also fosters eco-friendly agriculture, creating additional income through green crop cultivation. This comprehensive approach aids poverty alleviation while enabling women to stay close to their families.

Additionally, the Foundation organizes summer camps that enrich left-behind children’s lives through art, literature, and character development, fostering a nurturing environment for their growth and joy.

The Supporting Accompanying Mothers in County Towns initiative addresses the daily challenges these mothers face by providing:

  • Emotional Support: A compassionate community for sharing experiences and forming connections.
  • Practical Aid: Resources and guidance to enhance their children’s education.
  • Advocacy: Raising awareness and promoting better educational opportunities in rural areas.
  • Empowerment: Opportunities for personal growth and development.

Through these initiatives, the Lea Foundation seeks to empower mothers and foster positive family and community outcomes.
